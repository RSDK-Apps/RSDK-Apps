Bookmark Mega Suite

Bookmark Mega Suite is a Chrome browser extension that gives you total control over how you organize, customize, and access your bookmarks. View your collection in a floating window or directly inside the Chrome Side Panel. Personalize your bookmark tree with custom themes, clean up clutter with built-in tools, and protect sensitive links behind a private, password-protected folder.
